What Is a Local Cab Service in Mumbai and Who Needs It?

A local cab is a taxi used for travel inside city limits. It can be a single point to point ride, a trip with a few stops, or a car kept with you for several hours. People who use it most are office goers, senior citizens going for check-ups, students, families on errands, business visitors and tourists seeing the city. If you want a driver you can rely on without waiting for an app to find one, a local cab booking is the simple answer.
